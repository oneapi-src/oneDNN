# Quantized K/V coverage through the in-tree graph case (the --sdpa driver
# has no K/V scales or zero points). Tensor ids:
#   0 K [B,H,D,K]  1,2 K scales/zp [B,H,D/G,K]  3 Q [B,H,Q,D]  4 scale
#   5 mask [1,1,Q,K]  6 V [B,H,K,D]  7,8 V scales/zp [B,H,K,D/G]
#   15,16,18 KQ scores before and after scale and mask
# Ops 0 and 99 dequantize K and V. Overrides every line needs to reach the
# micro kernel (micro.hpp, sdp_primitive_config.cpp):
# - 4,15,16,18:f32: the graph SDPA primitive only takes f32 intermediates
# - B=2: K scale masks must be 0, 1, 3, 11 or 15, and B=1 drops dim 0
# int4 K/V is left out until the kernel is fixed on PVC: s4 zero points with
# V groups of 16 give wrong results from the second group on, int4 decode
# faults on the GPU, and about half the int4 prefill configs fail to run.
